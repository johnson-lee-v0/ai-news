# Five-layer research view

The view follows Jensen Huang's [March 10, 2026 framework](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ai-5-layer-cake/): energy, chips, infrastructure, models and applications. It organizes research questions; it does not certify NVIDIA's economic predictions.

Energy tracks delivered electricity, grid timing and cost. Chips tracks qualified shipments, memory and packaging. Infrastructure tracks utilization, pricing, capital spending and funding. Models tracks cost per successful task and reliability. Applications tracks paid adoption, retention, gross profit and customer returns.

Local tags match words in source titles and summaries. Public editorial mappings are explicit. A story can span several layers, so counts must not be added into a market total. Grouped coverage retains which appearance supplied each tag. Secondary text does not become a primary disclosure because the group has a primary representative.

Source health and article dates are separate. Failed or old sources do not appear connected; static references are not live endpoint checks. Measurements are marked unavailable because this app does not extract standardized operating metrics. Local dashboard refreshes recalculate the view from retained evidence. This feature installs no background service; local updates do not publish the public snapshot.

# Reading financial relationships

Each card shows the provider, recipient, instrument, amount and dates. The arrow describes what moves: equity, debt, customer commitments, capacity, credits and guarantees are different exposures. Revenue requires its own evidence; cash receipt is not automatically recognized revenue. The legend does not invent missing relationships.

Proposed funding, announced advances and planned capacity remain labeled as such. Shared overlap groups flag records describing one arrangement. No total combines instruments, units or stages. Unknown payment, recognition and termination terms remain unknown.

The Berlin review distinguishes the October 20–22 event and October 21 announcements from its October 25 review. Prepared-site installation excludes site development. Efficiency claims depend on workload and facility conditions; cooling water is not the complete water footprint. Targets and demonstrations do not establish delivered output, utilization or sales.
