# News and financing network

News is the first view. Latest orders selected stories by their supplied source date, with unknown or unverified future dates last. Important uses curated editorial order in the static snapshot and transparent relevance scores locally. Search and collapsed filters narrow this selection; All evidence under Explore retains background records. A note's review date is never used as its original publication date.

Cards show the headline and a short conditional implication. Evidence, claim types, counterevidence, confirmation/refutation tests and dated sources are expandable. News coverage remains a selected sample. The public site is a manually reviewed snapshot, not an automatic feed, even when opened today.

## Visit comparison

One path-scoped browser key stores at most 2,000 stable IDs and 16-character attention fingerprints, plus the baseline time. No source text, URLs, account data or private notes are saved in that key or transmitted. This is a convenience for attention, not cryptographic proof or a complete reading history. Clearing browser storage resets it.

The first visit establishes a baseline and labels nothing new. Later visits compare IDs and substantive content: whitespace, rankings, fetch timestamps, review timestamps, page builds and feed-date-only corrections do not count as new news. Group aliases prevent another appearance of the same story from being marked new. Here, a material revision means substantive source or curated text changed; the comparison does not establish financial materiality. Material edits to a known representative can be shown as revised; an unseen promoted representative first receives a baseline. The comparison stays fixed while navigating or refreshing within one visit. If browser storage is denied or full, the comparison reports unavailable.

## Reading the network

Full network means every relationship in this curated dataset, not all AI transactions. Choose a company to focus its relationships. Desktop arrows and the accessible list expose the same records; mobile has a company selector and readable detail sheet without pinch-to-read controls. Relationship links preserve company focus, recent-source filtering and detail state across browser back/forward and reload.

Arrows follow the explicitly sourced direction. Equity and debt funding, compute-purchase obligations, guarantees, ownership rights, reservation advances and service capacity have different economics. A provider-to-customer capacity arrow does not establish the amount or timing of a reverse cash payment. Each record retains dates, stage, payment/revenue status, uncertainty, source and overlap group. No network-wide dollar total is calculated.

Recent changes is a source-date window: the seven calendar days ending on the snapshot's review/as-of date. It is not a claim that each transaction is newly funded, and it never uses the date the page was opened. Historical, conditional and current records stay distinguishable.

The Chamath/Musk post is attributed commentary and an executive claim. The reported new $40 billion SpaceX financing has no independently verified closing document in this review and is excluded from confirmed debt relationships. Circular relationships alone do not establish fraud.
